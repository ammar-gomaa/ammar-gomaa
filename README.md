### 🧑‍💻 About me

- I'm a **frontend developer** with about 2 years of hands-on experience, mostly building production apps with a backend partner in a two-person team.
- I specialise in **Next.js App Router**, **React** and **TypeScript**, with a strong focus on clean architecture: feature-based structure, a **BFF proxy** with httpOnly cookie auth, and **TanStack Query** for server state.
- I work **spec-driven** (GitHub Spec Kit), **contract-first** against OpenAPI specs, and **mock-first** with MSW, so the frontend never waits on the backend.
- I'm self-taught, and I'm currently strengthening my CS foundations through structured study.
